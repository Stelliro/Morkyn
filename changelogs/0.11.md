# Mørkyn 0.11

The builds after the `0.10.0-dev` pre-releases. Latest: `0.11.3`. 0.11.1 was the first 0.11 build pushed to GitHub; 0.11.0 was a local build. Each push is the next patch (`0.11.4`, `0.11.5`), with its own section in [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

- **The Windows NVIDIA pack loads without the CUDA Toolkit (0.11.3).** The pack carries NVIDIA's CUDA runtime and cuBLAS and the game loads them before the model library; it had worked only on a PC with the CUDA Toolkit installed. Reinstall the pack from Updates, Local engine.
- **Money is coins, held as one number (0.11.2).** Each world has a currency set (copper, silver and gold by default, or credits, bottle caps, anything one-unit), picked at setup; the purse, prices and rewards are one number in the smallest coin, the engine does the math and shows it as coins. Money moves only by a sum the scene states, never by the die, and a sale moves the coin and the item together.
- **Jobs and creatures as the story shows them (0.11.2).** A job is filed when pay or work is offered, not from chatter or praise; a creature the story shows acting gets a record (Direwolf 1), so an attack at it lands on it and never on a bystander.
- **The cheat holes are closed (0.11.2).** A 100-turn run spent trying to talk the game into things found moves by declaration, one-sided trades, blows and kills the player declared, powers the player does not hold, codes taken as items and code travel; the engine now refuses each.
- **Setup fixes (0.11.2).** Setup boxes keep spaces and words as typed, hair keeps every piece, and a character image too large for the browser's storage no longer stops setup.

- **Unnamed people and creatures are kept (0.11.1).** A ghoul, raider or guard the scene shows acting gets its own record, numbered by kind (Ghoul 1, Ghoul 2); a group with no count is rolled once and the number written into the story and kept after. The dead are a 0/1 state the narrator is told only when it is 1, and they leave a body where they fell.
- **The story is told where the player is (0.11.1).** The narrator is told the player's place first; a place reached by leaving another is filed where the scene ended, not inside the place left; the people met are filed where the player stands.
- **One roll decides an attack (0.11.1).** The combat math follows the attack check rolled on the same person, a death the roll did not allow is refused, and gold leaves the purse only when the scene shows money change hands (checked twice). Hermes 3's op shapes are read as the op form.
- **Linux .deb playtest fixes (0.11.1).** The game runs in a terminal you can close, an older server is replaced instead of reused, the window no longer dies at start, the phone address comes as a QR code, world-scale map moves work, the Settlement view is only for the settlement you are in, and a rewind puts back everything the turn changed, with "Back to this turn" in History.

- **People are pencilled in (0.11.0).** Everyone a turn makes is a sketch until a later turn brings them back; twins made in one turn become one person, and a sketch that is the same person as someone known is merged into them. A sketch nobody returns to fades after five turns, and comes back as themselves if the story names them again.
- **Places have insides, jobs are filed as said (0.11.0).** A ship's hold or a tavern's back room is a place inside its vessel or building, not a new one beside it. A job is filed with the words it was offered in, its steps are written as play happens, and what you are owed is an engine ledger.
- **Acts from grammar, and the day's energy checked by replay (0.11.0).** The player's line is split into clauses and each judged by its form, so only what you do now rolls a check, costs energy or passes the clock (not speech, plans, or what waits on a condition). Energy costs were rebalanced against past runs replayed through the engine. A pronoun is pinned to a person only on a clear majority of the sentences about them, and its repair touches less.
- **Settings that stay put (0.11.0).** API keys and the model and speech settings save per user (encrypted on Windows) instead of inside a save. Settings that do nothing under another setting are greyed with the reason on hover, and the main menu's Gatehouse follows a save in LLM details.
- **Linux (0.11.0).** A .deb, an .rpm, an AppImage, an AUR PKGBUILD and a user install in the tarball, all sharing saves in `~/.local/share/morkyn`; the game's own window as a downloadable add-on (Qt WebEngine).
