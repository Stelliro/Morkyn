# Mørkyn 0.11

The builds after the `0.10.0-dev` pre-releases. Latest: `0.11.0`. Each push after it is the next patch (`0.11.1`, `0.11.2`), with its own section in [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

- **People are pencilled in (0.11.0).** Everyone a turn makes is a sketch until a later turn brings them back; twins made in one turn become one person, and a sketch that is the same person as someone known is merged into them. A sketch nobody returns to fades after five turns, and comes back as themselves if the story names them again.
- **Places have insides, jobs are filed as said (0.11.0).** A ship's hold or a tavern's back room is a place inside its vessel or building, not a new one beside it. A job is filed with the words it was offered in, its steps are written as play happens, and what you are owed is an engine ledger.
- **Acts from grammar, and the day's energy checked by replay (0.11.0).** The player's line is split into clauses and each judged by its form, so only what you do now rolls a check, costs energy or passes the clock (not speech, plans, or what waits on a condition). Energy costs were rebalanced against past runs replayed through the engine. A pronoun is pinned to a person only on a clear majority of the sentences about them, and its repair touches less.
- **Settings that stay put (0.11.0).** API keys and the model and speech settings save per user (encrypted on Windows) instead of inside a save. Settings that do nothing under another setting are greyed with the reason on hover, and the main menu's Gatehouse follows a save in LLM details.
- **Linux (0.11.0).** A .deb, an .rpm, an AppImage, an AUR PKGBUILD and a user install in the tarball, all sharing saves in `~/.local/share/morkyn`; the game's own window as a downloadable add-on (Qt WebEngine).
