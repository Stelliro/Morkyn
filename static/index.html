Welcome · first run

Before you build a world

The settings can look a little confronting at first — lots of knobs, locks, and options. We designed them for simple controls: change what you care about, leave the rest alone, or open Presets. That menu has two sides. Saved presets are built-in ideas and setups you keep with Save preset. Game starts are how a previous game began, not the middle of that save. New makes a text preset. Confirm preset fills the page from that idea, including powers, or loads a saved setup or a previous game’s opening. Then only edit what bothers you.

That said: these settings matter. Genre, tone, death rules, how NPCs behave, magic, and checks all teach the model what kind of story this is. You’re not filling paperwork for its own sake — you’re shaping how the world will play.

The next screens walk each important control with a spotlight. Take your time — use Back anytime.

Starting gear

Three worn basics are always here: feet, torso, legs. The model names and describes them for your world when you randomize; lock a card to keep it. Add anything else you carry. Stats and abilities on an item are optional; Roll stats lets the engine pick numbers from the world.

Powers

Optional starting abilities. Base text is locked once play begins.

None · add abilities or randomize

Add none, one, or several abilities. Lock an ability to keep it when you randomize; the others are rewritten, and the number of abilities stays. State on each card is whether it starts usable or needs a prerequisite. Growth type per card; Calculate level-up math fills numbers from world/difficulty. Final math also refreshes at Start.

Randomize is locked until the model has something to build on: add a world description, an idea, a backstory, or skill/race notes first.

Randomize ability count –

Min and max apply when the list is empty. Once abilities are here, randomize keeps that number: locked cards stay, the others are rewritten. Min does not add abilities. Max does not remove abilities you already have.

Power growth types (tips)
  • Compounding — weak start; each rank multiplies toward late OP (S/SS/SSS).
  • Passive — always-on aura/trait; can still rank up; no activate cost.
  • Linear — steady +% or +check per rank; predictable.
  • Soft-cap — grows normally then slows hard after a mid rank until a breakthrough.
  • Breakthrough — plateaus until a story beat; then jumps.
  • Flat — fixed power; no rank ladder (talent/item lock).
  • Item-bound — grows only while the linked gear is worn/used.

Help

Commands

Type these in the box. They edit the world. They do not play a scene. /help opens this panel.

/godmode on
Damage does not stick. /godmode off turns it off.
/teleport @L…
Move to that place now. Add a line if you want the model to be specific.
/stat health 30
Also max_health, gold, xp, level.
/item @I…
Then the change, such as “make the soles louder but keep them black”.
/npc @C…
Change that person.
/cast interacting @C…
Who replies. present is nearby and does not answer. off clears them.
/give rope 1
Put an item in the pack.
