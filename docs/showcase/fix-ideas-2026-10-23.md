### Lessons

- **The worktrees started from `origin/main` (c8c49f6, 2026-10-22), not local `main`**, 25 commits behind. Every fix was made and verified on day-old code. The two good fixes cherry-picked cleanly and the full suite on main matched the baseline. From round 2 on, each agent first resets its worktree to local main.
- **The three unmerged fixes rested on hand-written phrase lists** (death words; ~45 harm verbs, body parts and person words; a parse of the verifier's wording). The skeptics showed what CLAUDE.md predicts: ordinary phrasings missed (`slit`, `put a bullet in`, `kill` vs `killed`), false hits (`i cut the guard off mid-sentence` rolled a melee attack; `i hit the guard with a question`), and prose cut instead of state reconciled. Fix by class: candidates come from the engine's own records (every person and place code the turn names), and one small plain model ask decides.
- **One interpreter, not three detectors.** #154 (deaths), #129/#85 (outcome vs dice), #157 (memory) and #41 (gold taken with no payment) are one class: the turn's outcome is never stated as facts. CLAUDE.md already plans it (narrator, interpreter, engine). Round 2 builds its core; dice and gold build on its events in round 3.
- **New asks must log their prompt, reply and timing** in the turn trace; round 1's asks passed `trace=[]` / `usage=[]` like ask_weight and sketches, so a live failure could not be debugged.
- **A recorded death must be undoable** (rewind, and a later turn that shows a revival the world allows), or one false yes from the 8B kills an NPC for good.

**rewind**

- Store history_summaries once per change instead of in every record (it is copied whole into each of the 30 records), or keep only a hash plus a diff.
- Free map steps tick the weather and needs outside any turn. A small 'between turns' record, filled when the next turn starts with the keys changed since the last turn ended (configuration keys excluded), would let a rewind across map steps take the weather back too.
- Make TURN_START_WHOLE_TABLES the only place a module registers per-turn state: needs, body_health, restraint and economy each carry 'add to _save_snapshot' integration notes that a registry call could replace.
- Show on each History turn which records are kept (for example a faint 'cannot go back here' on turns older than REWIND_KEEP), so the player knows how far back they can go.
- Route popout-window clicks for .historyRewindButton back to the opener with postMessage, so History in a separate window can rewind too.

**facts**

- Extend the interpreter step to other status facts the same way: captured or tied up, fled or left town, badly wounded. Each would get one vocabulary class, one confirm ask and one row field.
- When the hold cut leaves a stub (T13), make one narrow re-ask of only the cut paragraph with the fact 'Beth is dead' and the player's line, instead of shipping a near-empty scene. Measure how often it happens first.
- Add a place hold: a destroyed place written as standing ('clinic gates' in T6) could get the same told checker ('The clinic was destroyed in turn 5. Does this passage show it standing whole?') and a cut of those sentences.
- Have the summary writer read the turn's recorded status facts, so a summary never says 'You killed Beth' over prose that did not (T13). This would also feed #157(a).
- Show the dead in the UI people list as greyed out with 'died turn N', and let the save editor clear a wrong death.
- Gate the record on the dice: when the turn's resolved check failed, ask the #129 cluster's outcome mechanism before recording a death. That keeps one source of truth for 'the dice did not allow it'.
- Merge the duplicate 'NAME: Beth' row (C) into B through the label-leak cluster's fix, so @Cbeth reaches the dead row and the hold covers it.

**memory-checker**

Does this prose have her alive?'). That would also cover #157 (c) and the T6 dead-Beth case.
- After a verifier patch, re-run the grounding against the patched prose: if an issue about the prose remains and narration was not patched (T8), mark it unresolved instead of passed.
- Turn the verifier's patch that changed the player's declared action (T9 laser rifle into a punch) into a player-agency check: a patch may not replace the act the player's line states.
- Feed turn_memory's prose-built line with the interpreter's stated events once the narrator/interpreter/engine plan lands, so the summary is the engine's event list instead of sentence picking.
- Move the summary row write after sketches.settle so code renames reach the stored summary too.

- Measure the summary_check ask's time per turn on the 8B; if it is too slow, ask only when the verifier or an engine cut changed the prose after the draft (the main cause of the mismatch).

**dice**

- Give a forceful act against a known person an opposed DC from their defense or power rank (the _check_with_opposition contested path), and push the line's combat intent so the deterministic combat engine resolves damage. T10 was classified 'travel'.
- Feed a successful attack check to #154 as the engine fact for 'X is down or dead': the death becomes a record the next turn is checked against, instead of a prose-only outcome.
- Extend check_outcome to social checks with one question per roll, e.g. 'Persuading Beth failed. Does this text show her agreeing?', for the 7B run 5 T93/T96 case.
- If the 8B often answers yes without quoting usable sentences, split the ask in two: yes/no first, then a separate 'which sentences' ask, keeping each ask simple.
- Hand prose_review results to the verifier memory or the next turn's summary so the corrected outcome, not the dropped one, is what later turns remember (#157a).

### What the skeptics said about the unmerged attempts (for whoever picks them up)

**memory-checker**

- A real disabled playthrough option can now have its violation dropped. verifier_grounding.rule_is_backed matches option keys by shared word stems, so a synonym ('no combat' when fighting_enabled=False), a forbidden act the prose shows without using the option's word ('no fighting' over 'You stab the guard'), or an unrelated key with a similar name ('no loot' matched to loot_rarity) is discarded as unbacked. Before this change the issue stood.
- When a revise verdict still has at least one backed issue, a dropped issue's patch is applied anyway. T9 replay: the inventory issue is dropped, but the patch that turns the laser-rifle attack into a punch and rewrites player.action is applied, and passed=true.
- A model summary replaced by the prose-built line can lose an outcome that comes late in the prose. T10: the built line ends at 'you attempt to rip her in two... Morning light filters in', so Beth's death is gone from turn_summaries (the #154 fault family).
- A pass verdict that lists any surviving issue now gives passed=false. That stops _write_verification_memory rows for those turns and shows 'needs review' in the UI. This is intended, but it changes behaviour on every pass-with-nitpicks turn and has not been checked live.
- The summary_check ask runs inside apply_turn's open connect().closing() write block, which can hold the sqlite write lock for up to its 60 s timeout. sketches already does this, but it is one more model call under the lock, and its usage and trace are thrown away (usage=[], trace=[]), so its time is in no timings or trace record.
- verifier_grounding's rule citation is a phrase parse: a quoted name within a word of setting/rule/option/restriction/policy. Other wordings of the same fault ('breaks the no killing setting' unquoted, "the game's rules against killing NPCs", 'Per the settings, magic is not allowed') are not caught. That fixes by phrasing, not by class, against CLAUDE.md 2026-10-14. The fixer labels it narrow, but not as a last resort.
- The item-lack drop is a regex over verifier wording (_LACKS_RE) plus a match on shared words with the player's line. Any line word of 4+ letters that also appears in a lack issue drops it, for example 'I look at the rifle on the wall'.
- The fix keeps the broad verifier and filters what it says afterwards, instead of making the task simpler (one question per fact), which is what the issue and CLAUDE.md 2026-10-17 ask for. Its patches still override the player's stated action (T9, T13) and speak for the player (T13), and nothing here checks that.
- turn_memory adds one new small model ask per model-written turn. It is short and plain, which is allowed under 2026-10-21/22, but it has not been checked live on the 8B, and the brief admits its yes-bias risk. Unless the reply is unusable, the engine's own word check never gets a say, so the engine fallback cannot overrule a yes-biased 'none'.
- No narrator prompt text was added. No content is banned or limited, and the T5 massacre draft now stands as the player wrote it, which fits the guiding rule.

**dice**

- T8 real line 'i shoot a missile at @Cbeth blowing her to bits' now rolls melee instead of ranged: forceful_act_on_person matches 'blowing her to bits' and _RANGED_VERB_RE reads only that piece (app/skill_checks.py, infer_check_from_action attack branch).
- T5 real line now rolls melee for 'snap her neck' instead of ranged. The gunning down of the city and the burning of the clinic, which the old ranged roll covered, now have no roll.
- New melee 'attack' rolls on ordinary lines that rolled nothing before: 'i burn the letter the woman gave me', 'i cut the old man a deal', 'i hit the stranger up for directions', 'i cut the guard off mid-sentence', 'i hit the guard with a question'. 'i break the man's fall' changed from athletics to a melee attack whose outcome words are 'it lands' / 'the person is not harmed as intended'.
- The check's 'for' and the outcome ask's 'attempt' now carry the lowercased model_input with engine codes, e.g. 'shoot at beth [[b]]' (T7). Before, it was the player's line ('i shoot at @Cbeth'). A lowercase code in the narrator packet risks codes leaking into the prose (minor).
- When a cut is made but the success is in the scene's first sentence, the ledger note unearned_success_dropped is logged while the success sentence ships.
- Fix by phrasing, not by class: the trigger is a hand-written list of about 45 harm verbs, a body-part list and a person-word list. It misses common kill phrasings (slit, wring, twist off, set on fire, blast, put a bullet in, end her life, pull apart, cave in, squeeze until she stops) and misfires on idioms (cut a deal, hit up, cut off, burn the letter the woman gave me). That is phrase-level work, which CLAUDE.md (2026-10-14) calls a last resort, and it was not labelled as one.
- The repair is a gate that removes prose (drop_sentences) and does not reconcile the state. The journal, turn_summary and events still record the outcome the dice refused, so prose and state disagree, which is the fault class CLAUDE.md names first. 'Engine first' would have the engine refuse the death/murder records when the attack check failed (the narrator / interpreter / engine plan), not only cut sentences.
- No narrator prompt line was added: the prompts.py change only swaps the wording of the existing 'means' row (_ACT_OUTCOME_WORDS to outcome_means). That is within the rules.
- The new small ask is short and asks one question, which fits the 2026-10-21 rule. But it relies on the 8B copying sentences word for word, a task local models often fail. Under the 2026-10-17 'make the task simpler' rule, asking for sentence numbers from a numbered list would be easier for the model and easier to match.
- The ask's usage and trace are thrown away (usage=[], trace=[], as in ask_weight and sketches). Its time and its prompt/reply do not reach model_usage or the turn trace, against 'log everything a bug needs'. Only a 200-character raw sample is kept, and only when the ask fails.

**facts**

- The hold asks one question per dead person and, on one yes, cuts every grammar-flagged sentence together. That includes corpse descriptions and the player's own act sentences ('You kneel beside Beth [[B]], her eyes still open.' and 'Beth [[B]] lies where she fell, motionless.' are flagged as acts), so a single yes about one living line can take out the player's own act.
- A recorded death cannot be undone except by rewind. A false yes from the 8B on a candidate such as 'Beth [[B]] kneels beside the corpse of a raider' or 'Beth [[B]] killed the raider' (both are candidates) leaves an NPC dead for good, and every later line where she acts is cut.
- A death written by someone else in a two-person sentence is pinned on the wrong person ('Jonah grabs Beth. She dies in his arms.' makes Jonah the candidate), so the ask can be asked about the wrong NPC.
- Matches one phrasing: DEATH_RE/RUIN_RE carry run-specific phrases ('(ripped|torn|tears|rips) ... (apart|in half|in two)', 'life has been ripped', 'neck twisted', 'melting ... to nothing', 'reduced to'). The basic class words are missing ('kill', 'kills', 'execute', 'collapses', 'explodes', 'burn X to the ground' with the object in between). The candidate step is phrase-fitted to cc87dd29 rather than built for the class.
- The grammar-based act finder in act_sentences is a large hand-built regex set (title words, clause openers, quote tracking). It already misses inverted speech tags and unnamed speakers, so more phrase rules are likely to pile up there, which is the outcome the 2026-10-14 'fix by class, not by phrasing' decision was meant to avoid.
- Gate rather than reconcile: on yes, the hold cuts sentences with no retry and no reconcile. In T13 the turn shrinks to one 84-char sentence. The rule allows a gate for facts that contradict the state, but cutting several sentences on one yes, including corpse descriptions and the player's own acts, comes close to limiting the prose.
- No way back: a recorded death is permanent outside rewind. Raising the dead, ghouls, or simply a mistaken record is then gated forever, which limits the model and the player.
- No narrator prompt instruction was added, which complies. The facts go through the existing facts_to_keep data line.
